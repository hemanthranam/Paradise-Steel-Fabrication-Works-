About Us

Established in 1989, Paradise Steel Fabrication Works specialises in steel fabrication and construction material-hoist manufacturing, serving contractors and civil projects from Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh.

Reliable Vertical Material Handling Engineered for Demanding Job Sites

Under the leadership of Founder R. Rama Krishna, PSFW has spent over three decades perfecting high-duty construction material hoists that replace slow, injury-prone manual carrying with rapid, mechanized vertical delivery.

Direct Factory Engineering Support

Every SPEED LIFT machine is fabricated, assembled, and test-run directly at Plot E-13, Industrial Development Estate, Madanapalle before shipment to client job sites.

Heavy-Duty Structural Truss

Fabricated from prime structural steel channels and cross-braced modular masts for robust field resilience under continuous lifting shifts.

Integrated Magnetic Brake

Instant failsafe electro-mechanical holding brake locks the winch drum in place when power drops or controls are released, preventing load slip.

360° Slew Bucket Discharge

Smooth counterbalanced material hopper with 360-degree radial pivot allows effortless, safe slab unloading without workers leaning over slab edges.

Why Building Contractors Choose Paradise Steel

Construction schedules require equipment that survives harsh site conditions—cement dust, abrasive sand, continuous hoisting cycles, and extreme weather.

  • 01 Heavy Box-Truss Mast Engineering
    Heavy-gauge structural channel columns with diagonal lacing provide high torsional rigidity, ensuring stable bucket guidance up to 10 floors without excessive mast deflection.
  • 02 Fail-Safe Magnetic Safety Braking
    Spring-applied, electrically released magnetic disc brake automatically locks the winch drum the instant power is cut or the operator releases the hoist switch.
  • 03 Direct Factory Spares & Fast Support
    Because we are the original equipment manufacturer in Madanapalle, replacement wire ropes, brake friction discs, sheaves, and motor assemblies ship directly with zero distributor markup or delay.
