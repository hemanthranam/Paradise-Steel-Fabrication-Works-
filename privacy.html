Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Paradise Steel Fabrication Works ("we", "our", or "the Company"), having its manufacturing facility at Plot No. E-13, Industrial Development Estate, Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh – 517325, is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of visitors, contractors, and customers who visit our website or submit business inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly necessary for responding to commercial machinery inquiries and providing technical quotations. This includes:

  • Lead & Contact Details: Full name, telephone/mobile number, email address, company or business trade name.
  • Site & Project Requirements: Delivery location/city, electrical supply specifications (single-phase vs three-phase), number of building floors, intended material lifting payload, and expected dispatch dates.
  • Direct Communications: Inquiries sent via WhatsApp Click-to-Chat, email messages sent to manager.psfw@gmail.com, or telephone conversations with our sales engineering personnel.

3. Processing via Google Apps Script & Google Sheets

When you submit a quotation inquiry form on our website, the data is encrypted and transferred via HTTPS POST to our private Google Apps Script backend. The script writes the inquiry to a secured, access-restricted Google Sheet maintained solely by authorized Paradise Steel managerial staff. A concurrent email notification containing your inquiry reference number is sent to our management desk at manager.psfw@gmail.com.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your inquiry details with third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or automated telemarketers.

4. WhatsApp Interactions

Our website provides direct links to chat with our engineering desk on WhatsApp (+91 94405 85329). Any interaction conducted over WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption and terms of service. Information exchanged on WhatsApp (such as drawings, floor plans, and quotation documents) is retained solely for business fulfillment.

5. Cookies & Website Logs

This website does not deploy invasive tracking cookies or commercial behavioral ad trackers. Standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and browser headers) may be recorded by our static hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages) for security auditing, DDoS mitigation, and server performance monitoring.

6. Data Retention & Security

Inquiry records are retained in our internal business registers for customer relationship management, warranty verification, and machine service tracking. We implement strict administrative controls, role-based access limits, and secure password management to prevent unauthorized access.

7. User Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about your inquiry, request corrections, or request that your contact details be deleted from our quotation register. To exercise these rights, please email us directly at manager.psfw@gmail.com with your inquiry reference number.
