Site Utility

What Builders Transport with SPEED LIFT

Engineered to handle the standard high-density bulk materials required for structural masonry and slab casting.

Concrete & Mortar

Hoists rubber buckets and wheelbarrows of concrete straight to masons on upper floors without delay.

Red Bricks & AAC Blocks

Lifts bulk brick batches without chipping or breakage, keeping wall construction progressing rapidly.

River Sand & Aggregate

Eliminates manual head-carrying of heavy sandbags and aggregate, preventing worker fatigue.

Tiles, Granite & Marble

Smooth, controlled hoisting for delicate finishing materials, preventing broken slabs and fragile item losses.

Piping & Steel Hardware

Rapid vertical shift of heavy CPVC piping bundles, conduit, electrical cables, and distribution boxes.

Debris & Rubble Lowering

Supports controlled lowering cycles to safely bring construction rubble down to ground collection bins.
