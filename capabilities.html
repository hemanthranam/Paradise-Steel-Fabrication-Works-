Selection Guidance

Which Model Suits Your Construction Site?

Choose based on your typical load sizes and pour frequencies.

Choose SPEED LIFT 250 if:

Your site primarily hoists mortar pans, bricks, wall tiles, and bagged cement for residential projects.

  • Building height is between 1 and 10 floors.
  • Typical bucket loads range between 150 kg to 250 kg.
  • Site requires rapid setup, compact footprint, and easy slab mobility.
  • Standard 1 HP electric motor provides optimal balance of speed and power draw.
Explore SPEED LIFT 250
Commercial Heavy Duty

Choose SPEED LIFT 500 if:

You are pouring high-volume concrete or lifting dense granite, marble, steel, or heavy pallets.

  • Commercial multi-story projects with tight deadlines.
  • Heavier load batches up to 500 kg per hoisting cycle.
  • Continuous duty cycles requiring reinforced truss and heavy base frame.
  • Heavy-duty magnetic braking for maximum safety with double payload.
Explore SPEED LIFT 500
