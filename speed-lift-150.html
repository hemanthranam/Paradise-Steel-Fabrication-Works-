Equipment Data Sheet

Paradise Steel Fabrication Works • Madanapalle
150 kg Payload
Lifting Capacity: 150 kg (Manufacturer benchmark payload)
Lifting Reach: Up to 60 ft (~4-5 Floors) (Residential reach)
Power Supply: Single Phase (220V, 50 Hz) domestic line ready
Safety Braking: Electromagnetic holding brake locks drum on power stop
Structural Frame: Ultra-compact tubular steel mast with quick-anchoring outriggers
Operator Controls: Directional push-button control pendant for simple floor operation
Recommended Materials: Mortar pans, red bricks, AAC blocks, ceramic tiles, plumbing kits
Maintenance: Accessible grease points, standard bearings, minimal electrical complexity
* Dedicated strictly to material handling. Human transit strictly prohibited.
