Industrial Legacy

Precision Engineering & Machinery Since 1989

Founded in Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh by R. Rama Krishna, Paradise Steel Fabrication Works has been designing, fabricating, and supplying robust material handling systems for more than three decades.

Company Background

Engineering Machines that Solve Real Job Site Realities

Building construction in India faces two continuous bottlenecks: spiraling manual labor costs and project delays caused by slow, fatigue-prone physical lifting of heavy materials across upper slabs.

At Paradise Steel Fabrication Works, our workshop in the Industrial Development Estate of Madanapalle engineered the SPEED LIFT series to solve this specific problem. Built from heavy structural steel, counterbalanced radial arms, and industrial-grade high-torque electric motors, our lifts make vertical material shifting seamless, rapid, and secure.

Each SPEED LIFT is manufactured under direct engineering oversight with precision welding, branded electrical components, and heavy-duty wire rope drums capable of lifting sand, mortar, concrete, bricks, and structural components safely.

Engineering Principles

Built on Five Core Fabricator Commitments

Verified from our official brochure flyer and workshop operational standards.

Saves Time & Labor Forever

Eliminates manual head-load carrying by replacing up to 5 laborers per shift, protecting workforce energy and speeding slab casting cycles.

Easy to Operate

Intuitive electrical controls and dual-direction winch drum allow any construction worker to be trained on safe hoist operation in under 15 minutes.

Easy for Erecting & Shifting

Engineered with modular structural sections (tare weight approx 155 kg) that can be easily dismantled, transported in a mini truck, and re-erected on the next floor or site.

Low Power Consumption

Equipped with high-efficiency 1.5 HP motors that can operate on standard domestic single-phase or commercial three-phase site power feeds without overloading transformers.

360° Slew Radial Unloading

Full radial rotation ensures that lifted buckets are swung completely onto the building slab before material discharge, guaranteeing zero fall danger for workers.

1-Year Full Motor Warranty

Should any damage occur, the motor is replaced with a brand-new unit, supported by frequent site health checkups and responsive after-sales service.
