Industrial Heritage

Precision Engineering Since 1989

Founded in Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh by R. Rama Krishna, Paradise Steel Fabrication Works designs, manufactures, and services rugged vertical material handling machinery for construction professionals.

Company Background

Engineering Machinery for Real Construction Demands

Civil construction sites in India face high labor turnover, elevated manual handling costs, and project delays when carrying materials up multi-story slabs.

At Paradise Steel Fabrication Works, our workshop in the Industrial Development Estate of Madanapalle engineered the SPEED LIFT machine series (SPEED LIFT 250 & SPEED LIFT 500) to solve this challenge directly. Constructed from heavy-duty structural steel, counterbalanced radial jibs, and magnetic braking safety drives, our machinery makes vertical lifting predictable and safe.

Manufacturing Values

Core Machinery Principles

Built directly into every SPEED LIFT unit leaving our Madanapalle workshop.

Magnetic Safety Braking

Failsafe electromagnetic braking system that locks the wire drum instantly when control buttons are released, preventing load slippage.

360° Slew Reach

Counterbalanced rotating boom swings full buckets onto the slab surface with zero hazard to terrace edge workers.

Simple Maintenance

Standardized mechanical joints, heavy-gauge steel channel frame, and easily replaceable bearings minimize site downtime.
