Commercial Policy

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Nature of Website Inquiries

Paradise Steel Fabrication Works manufactures heavy construction machinery and industrial equipment. This website is an informational catalogue and quotation request portal. Website inquiries and quotation requests are not financial transactions or purchases. No direct online monetary transactions are conducted through this website.

2. Commercial Order Confirmation & Contracts

All purchases of SPEED LIFT 250, SPEED LIFT 500, spare parts, or Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) are governed by specific commercial agreements:

  • The itemized formal quotation or proforma invoice issued by the Company.
  • The written purchase order submitted and signed by the purchasing contractor or customer.
  • Mutually signed sales agreements detailing delivery milestones, payment stages, and fabrication specifications.

3. Advance Payments & Custom Fabrication Cancellation

Because construction hoists and structural steel equipment are custom configured, assembled, and welded to specific site height and power requirements:

  • Before Fabrication Begins: Cancellations requested prior to procurement of raw materials and cutting may be eligible for partial refund of advance funds, less administrative and drafting expenses.
  • After Fabrication Commences: Once steel cutting, structural truss welding, or custom motor winding has been initiated for an order, advance deposits are non-refundable as materials are committed to the project.

4. Equipment Inspection & Acceptance

Customers and contractors are encouraged to inspect equipment at our Madanapalle workshop facility prior to final dispatch. Upon taking delivery and sign-off at site, the equipment is deemed accepted as conforming to the agreed structural specifications.

5. Motor Warranty & Replacement

In place of monetary returns, our machinery is protected by our manufacturer warranty:

  • SPEED LIFT units carry a comprehensive 1-year manufacturer warranty on the electric motor drive.
  • If any manufacturing defect or internal electrical failure occurs under rated operational loads, the motor will be replaced with a brand-new unit free of charge in accordance with warranty terms.
