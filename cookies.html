Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a web browser when you visit a website. They allow websites to remember basic technical state and operate securely.

2. How We Use Cookies

Paradise Steel Fabrication Works maintains a clean, privacy-respecting website. We do not use advertising, marketing, or behavioral tracking cookies.

The only technical cookies or browser storage mechanisms that may be utilized are:

  • Essential Technical Cookies: Strictly required to operate core features such as keeping modal dialogue states, form submission tokens, and offline caching via our service worker.
  • Third-Party Embedded Scripts: When you click our WhatsApp button or telephone links, your browser interacts with the respective communication platforms which may set their own session cookies under their privacy policies.

3. Controlling Cookies in Your Browser

You can configure your browser to reject all cookies or notify you whenever a cookie is offered. Most browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences through their settings menu (Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies; Safari: Preferences > Privacy; Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security).

4. Inquiries Regarding Cookies

If you have questions about our technical cookie practices, please reach out to us at manager.psfw@gmail.com.
