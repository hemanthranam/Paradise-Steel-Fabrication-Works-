Long-term Reliability

After-Sales Services & AMC Maintenance

Our relationship doesn’t end when your SPEED LIFT is delivered. We provide rapid service dispatch, guaranteed 1-year motor replacement, and budget-friendly Annual Maintenance Contracts.

Verified Commitments

Manufacturer Service Guarantees

From the official Paradise Steel service charter documented in our product brochure.

Frequent Machine Health Checkups

Our service technicians conduct scheduled periodic site visits to inspect boom alignment, brake shoe wear, cable tension, and grease points to prevent unpredicted downtime.

1-Year Motor Warranty

We provide a comprehensive 1-year warranty on the 1.5 HP electric motor. If any structural or winding damage occurs under normal usage, it will be replaced with a brand-new motor.

Minimal Electrical Part Charges

Direct-from-factory pricing on electrical component replacements including QA units, electrical panel boards, contactors, limits, and directional push buttons.

24x7 Customer Care Availability

Our service desk and proprietor lines remain open 24x7 to assist project site supervisors with electrical troubleshooting, voltage checks, and operational advice.

24-Hour Complaint Resolution

After registering a service call or breakdown complaint, our regional technician dispatches promptly with a target of closing issues within 24 hours on site.

Continuous Performance Feedback

We stay in close regular contact with our builders and civil engineers to collect feedback on lifting cycle speed, cable durability, and ongoing project requirements.
